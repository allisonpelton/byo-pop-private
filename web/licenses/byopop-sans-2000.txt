Byopop Sans 2000 (the site's text in the 2000 skin), drawn by the byo-pop project: CC0 1.0 Universal

Byopop Sans 2000 and its bold are the byo-pop project's own drawing (tools/web/pixelfont2000.py): pixel fonts in the
manner of Tahoma at 8 points, every glyph drawn by hand.
