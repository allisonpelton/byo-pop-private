#! repository (docs/PUBLISHING.md, "Derived repositories"; the manifest's rewords line). The trunk keeps its own wording:
#! tools/publish.py applies each entry to the published copy of its file, before it decides the conditionals. An entry
#! is "@@ <path>" ("@@ <path> x<n>" for lines that occur n times), the trunk's lines exactly as they are, "@to" and the
#! lines that replace them (none: the old lines go). The publish stops when an entry's old lines are no longer in its
#! file as often as it says, so a trunk edit cannot skip one: change the entry with the trunk's new text. The forbid
#! lines of the manifest catch a new comment that needs an entry. Lines starting "#!" are notes; blank lines at the end
#! of an entry's lines are not part of them.
