# Mod ideas for Bonnie's Bookstore Deluxe

A list only: no mod is made without the user's approval, and none before every PopCap game works in the browser. Each
would be configurable, never an overwrite of the original's behaviour.

- **HD art.** The game draws 800x600 software frames; an HD path would need the engine's blitter to draw at a render
  scale (as the framework does for SexyAppFramework games), then upscaled backgrounds and tiles.
- **Wide screen.** The bookstore and book backgrounds extended to 16:9, the board centred.
- **Dictionary options.** A setting to choose the word list (the game's, or a larger one the player supplies) and to
  show a found word's definition source.
- **Turn and goal tunables.** The classic game's turns per level and the action game's timer as settings (with their
  own high scores).
- **Level select from the start.** The new game's level chooser (already in the game, limited to the reached level)
  open to every book.
- **Hint button.** Highlight a valid word on the board, at a score cost, for younger players.
- **Colour-blind tile marks.** The special tiles (bonus, blockers) told apart by a shape as well as a colour.
- **Remembered mute.** The music and sound volumes kept per player rather than in the shared preferences.
